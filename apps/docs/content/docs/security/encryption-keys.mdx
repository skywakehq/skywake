title: Encryption keys
icon: LockKeyhole

Use your own encryption key when you need to control access to your data yourself. If you revoke the key in your cloud's key service, the data becomes unreadable.

## The whole bucket

To encrypt everything with your own KMS key, disks and their search data included, set that key as the bucket's default encryption. Use it with [your own bucket](../self-hosting/your-bucket.mdx) or on your own servers. On the [Helm chart](../self-hosting/kubernetes.mdx), the pods' role then also needs `kms:Encrypt`, `kms:Decrypt` and `kms:GenerateDataKey` on the key.
